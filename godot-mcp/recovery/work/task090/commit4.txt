modules/mcp_server: task090 (2c-9) step4 - a deferred call's own body reaches its trace line too

TASK-090 item C, the fourth round-8 defect (found after the first three were
fixed). `MCPTrace::Record::result_json` - TASK-089's "the tool's own answer" -
was filled only on the immediate success path, so every deferred call carried
`result_bytes` and nothing else. The scenario and stress drivers are exactly the
tools whose answer *is* a verdict (`all_passed`, `passed` per step), so the
ledger could not answer "did the assertions hold" for them no matter how the
reader was written.

* mcp_http_server.cpp `_tick_pending`: for `CompletionKind::DONE` the record now
  gets `result_json` / `result_json_bytes` from `completion.result` - the same
  Variant `build_deferred_body` wraps with the same `content_result`, so the
  deferred line and the wire body carry the same bytes.

Measured on the final round-8 battery: game `seq=25` carries
`"all_passed":true, "passed":1` (and the input step's `"in_input_map":true`),
`seq=26` carries `"all_passed":false, "failed":1`; the ledger now prints
`scenario_passed` and `scenario_assertion_failed` on those two rows.
