modules/mcp_server: task090 (2c-9) step3 - the round-8 fixes: the InputMap fact, the scenario flags, the frame-based deadline

TASK-090 item C. The round-8 session (a purpose-built mini game, both endpoints,
trace + every-call capture, 57 calls) produced three defects with a clear root
cause; this commit carries the two that are the module's.

1. `running_game_run_test_scenario`'s input step now answers `action` +
   `in_input_map`, the same field `editor_simulate_input_action` already answers.
   Root cause, measured and then read out of the engine source: the injected
   event *is* delivered to `_input` (the before-run's stderr carries the
   GDScript backtrace of `main.gd:_input`), but `InputEvent::is_action_pressed`
   resolves through `InputMap::event_get_action_status`, which answers false for
   an action the InputMap does not declare (core/input/input_map.cpp:291-292).
   The step reported `injected: 1` and the game silently did nothing.

2. `scripts/mcp_trace_ledger.py`'s `result_flags` also reads a scenario summary
   (`scenario_passed` / `scenario_assertion_failed` / `scenario_errors` /
   `scenario_asserted_nothing`). Without it the ledger could not answer the one
   question the scenario tools exist for - did the assertions hold - because a
   scenario nests its verdicts in `results[]` and summarises in `all_passed`.

3. Both drivers of `running_game_test_execution.cpp` estimated their deadline
   from a hard-coded frame time (250 ms per scenario step, 16 ms per stress
   iteration) although they advance one step / iteration per frame. Measured: a
   game run whose every call took 250-530 ms (a ~4 fps loop) killed a 0.4 s,
   3-step scenario that did exactly what it was asked with its own 1150 ms
   estimate. The estimate now uses the process' own frame rate, floored at the
   old constants, so a 60 fps game keeps the deadline it always had.

The third defect's harness half (the project declares `mcp_right` in its
InputMap) lives in H:\rebuild\projects\mcpplay8, outside the repository.
REBUILT-2C-MANIFEST.md section 2c-9 (J-1..J-3) records all of it, including the
two findings that are deliberately *not* defects (the capture's threshold-10
pixel rule, and the deferred channel's declared unobservability).
